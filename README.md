## Business Questions

- Which shift has the longest customer wait times?
- Which shift has the highest average picking time?
- Which shift has the most late orders?
- What factors contribute to fulfillment delays?
- How can retail fulfillment operations improve?

## Key Findings

### Customer Wait Times

- The evening shift had the highest late-order rate at 62.96%, with an average customer wait time of 12.17 minutes.
- Evening fulfillment performance may require further investigation.

### Picking Performance

- The afternoon shift had the highest average picking time at 23.25 minutes.
- Picking efficiency may be affected by order complexity and staffing.

### Late Orders

- Afternoon shift had the highest number of late orders.
- Total late orders and delay percentages should be compared to account for different order volumes.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate staffing levels during peak hours.
- Evaluate picking workflows to identify inefficiencies.
- Monitor fulfillment KPIs across all shifts.
- Compare order volumes with delay rates.
